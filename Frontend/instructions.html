SYSTEM GUIDE & SETTINGS

Simple instructions to configure your camera, faces, and email alerts.

1 Account Registration

First, register your operator account in Register and sign in to get access to the dashboard.

2 Gmail Alert Setup (Google App Password)

To allow WatchmanX to automatically send stranger alert photos to your inbox, you must generate a Google App Password:

1. Open Google Security: Visit your myaccount.google.com/security and ensure 2-Step Verification is switched ON.
2. Create App Password: Search for App Passwords in the top search bar inside your Google Account settings.
3. Enter Name: Type WatchmanX as the app name and click Create.
4. Copy Key: Google will show a 16-character code (e.g. abcd efgh ijkl mnop). Paste this key inside your backend email_alert.go as your sender password.
Important: Do not enter your normal Gmail account password. Google only allows sending automated emails through 16-character App Passwords.
3 Save Known Faces (Add Face)

Go to Add New Face. Enter the person's name and details. Look straight into the webcam and trigger scan. The biometric vector is safely stored in PostgreSQL.

4 Start Surveillance Feed

Open Scanning Mode, ensure your alert email is set up, and click Initialize Feed to start the primary camera.

5 Live Camera Rules
✔ KNOWN PERSON (SAFE)

Green box with name and role. Safe entry, no alert is triggered.

✖ UNKNOWN STRANGER (ALERT)

Red box with UNKNOWN warning. The camera captures their face and emails it directly to you within seconds!
